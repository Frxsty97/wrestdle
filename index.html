      How to play

      1. Hit play to hear the first second of a wrestler's entrance music.
      2. Guess the wrestler. Start typing a name and pick from the list.
      3. Skip or guess wrong to hear more: 1s → 2s → 4s → 7s → 11s → 16s.
      4. A wrong guess turns amber if it's a wrestler from the same promotion and era.
      5. Easy mode: each wrong wrestler guess shows clue chips — gender, singles vs. group, and company overlap — so you can home in faster. Stats are kept separately.
      6. Daily: one theme a day, the same for everyone. Unlimited: play as many as you like and filter by promotion or era.
